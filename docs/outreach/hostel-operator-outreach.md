# Lodgist — Hostel Operator Outreach Pack
## Eziobodo / Ihiagwa / Nekede corridor (FUTO + Federal Poly Nekede)

**Purpose:** Sign the first 3–5 authorized hostel operators so Lodgist has real, verifiable
inventory. The brief's pilot gate is 20 authorized adverts in one area. The open web has ~12
for all of Owerri Municipal — you cannot import your way to 20. Direct supply deals are the
only path.

**Why this corridor:**
- FUTO (Ihiagwa) + Federal Poly Nekede = two large student populations within ~20 min
- Eziobodo is the densest student area, closest to FUTO main gate
- On-campus FUTO hostel is ₦18,000–₦62,500/session and oversubscribed
- Private lodges run ₦300K–₦1.8M/year — a 10x–60x gap where students get exploited
- AcadEstate has exactly 1 listing here (Ace Lodge FUTO, ₦1.8M/yr) — the field is open

## 2. WHAT YOU ARE OFFERING (say this plainly)

You are **not** asking them to pay. You are offering:

1. **Free listing** — their hostel on Lodgist with photos, price, and location
2. **Students who arrive pre-qualified** — renters who have already seen the price,
   the area, and what was checked before they message
3. **A viewing request inbox** — they get a durable reference per request, no lost DMs
4. **No commission on rent** — Lodgist is not collecting rent or taking a cut of it
5. **A verified badge they earn** — by letting you confirm identity + authority to advertise

The honest pitch: *"Students don't trust listings because anyone can post anything. We show
what we actually checked. You get renters who show up ready, not 40 people asking the price."*

**What you ask for in return:**
- Permission to publish the listing (written/WhatsApp confirmation is fine to start)
- Their relationship to the property (owner / agent / manager) and authority to advertise
- Real photos they have the right to share
- Price components: rent, deposit, agency fee, service charge, utilities
- A response to viewing requests within a stated window

## 4. WHAT TO CAPTURE ON THE VISIT (checklist)

Take this on your phone. This becomes the listing.

**Identity & authority**
- [ ] Operator name, phone, WhatsApp
- [ ] Relationship: owner / agent / manager / tenant-sublet
- [ ] Authority evidence seen (ID, ownership doc, owner's written consent) — note what, don't photograph sensitive docs into your camera roll
- [ ] If agent: disclosed fee and who pays it

**The property**
- [ ] Lodge name
- [ ] Exact area (Eziobodo / Ihiagwa / Nekede / other)
- [ ] Landmark + approximate coordinates (drop a pin, don't guess)
- [ ] Room types available (single / self-contain / shared / 2-bed)
- [ ] Beds per room, bathroom sharing, kitchen sharing
- [ ] Occupancy policy as they state it (mixed / female only / male only)

**Money — get every component separately**
- [ ] Rent (state the period: per year / per session / per month)
- [ ] Deposit
- [ ] Agency fee
- [ ] Legal / agreement fee
- [ ] Service charge
- [ ] Utilities (power, water — who pays, how much)
- [ ] Any other disclosed charge
- [ ] Mark anything they don't know as UNKNOWN — do not estimate

**Facilities — as they claim, with the date**
- [ ] Power: grid / generator / solar; hours per day as claimed
- [ ] Water: borehole / tank / municipal
- [ ] Security: gate, guard, hours
- [ ] WiFi: yes/no, provider
- [ ] Furnished: what's included

**Media**
- [ ] 5–10 photos: exterior, room, bathroom, kitchen, water source, gate
- [ ] Written permission to publish the photos
- [ ] Note the date taken

**Journey**
- [ ] Walking time to FUTO gate / Poly Nekede gate
- [ ] Transport mode and cost

to operators so they understand the badge is earned, not bought. It also protects you — you
never overstate what you checked.

## 6. OBJECTION HANDLING

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already get tenants by word of mouth" | "This adds to that. It's free and it's another door." |
| "How much does it cost?" | "Listing is free. We don't take rent commission. Paid promotion may come later and it's optional." |
| "Will you collect the rent?" | "No. You collect rent directly. We only handle the viewing request." |
| "Why do you need my ID?" | "For the verified badge. Students won't pay attention to unverified listings. It stays private — nothing sensitive is public." |

| "What if I don't respond to requests?" | "Then the listing goes stale and we mark availability unknown. Responding is what keeps it live." |

## 8. WHAT NOT TO DO

- Do not scrape NEPC / PropertyPro / Private Property into production. Their terms do not
  grant ingestion rights. Credit is not permission.
- Do not publish a listing as verified before the check is actually done.
- Do not estimate a price component the operator didn't give you — mark it unknown.
- Do not promise safety, escrow, or fraud protection. You don't have the operational
  backing for that yet.
- Do not take money for verification. Payment must never guarantee approval.
- Do not present mock/example listings as real supply.
